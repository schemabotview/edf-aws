# Business needs

## On screen

## Business needs

EDF needs trustworthy reporting and current operational visibility across its metering, generation, trading and customer estate.

- **Regulatory confidence** — Reduce Ofgem reporting risk with reconciled, traceable datasets and auditable release decisions.
- **Reliable billing** — Detect meter validation and reconciliation failures before billing; reduce corrections and customer complaints.
- **Current operations** — Give teams a timely view of meter, grid and generation anomalies.
- **Commercial insight** — Provide consistent consumption, trading and customer analytics instead of stale extracts.

**Business need:** Improve freshness and correctness while preserving deterministic batch reporting.

## Narration

The business needs follow directly from the legacy problems. Regulatory teams need accepted datasets they can explain and reproduce. Billing teams need meter failures detected before a bill is issued, with corrections represented consistently. Operations needs current meter and grid events for anomaly investigation. Commercial teams need common definitions across consumption, trading and customer analysis. These needs require both timely event processing and deterministic reporting. Success is judged at the consumer boundary: a trustworthy report, a reconciled billing population, or a current operational view.
