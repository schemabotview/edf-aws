## Narration

EDF Energy operates a national smart metering, generation asset, and customer analytics estate spanning millions of domestic and commercial meters, generation telemetry feeds, and trading systems. Before this programme, meter, billing, and asset data arrived from a dozen heterogeneous source systems into a legacy on-premise estate with no consistent Bronze, Silver, Gold discipline. Regulatory Ofgem reporting datasets were hand-assembled by analysts from a mix of extracts, and commercial teams routinely worked from data that was twenty-four to forty-eight hours stale.

Meter reconciliation failures were common. Roughly six to eight percent of daily meter reads failed downstream validation each month, usually discovered only after billing had already run, triggering costly billing corrections and customer complaints. Grid event and generation asset telemetry was similarly batch-only. Operational teams had no way to see meter or grid anomalies until the next morning’s report, by which point any settlement or safety issue had already propagated.

The business case for the modernisation programme was clear: reduce Ofgem regulatory reporting risk, bring meter and billing reconciliation error rates down, and give operational teams a current view of smart metering and grid events without destabilising the deterministic, auditable batch pipeline regulators require.
